# 🚆 RailEase — User Journey Map

## Journey Overview

The journey maps the experience of a passenger from searching for a train to managing the journey after booking.

---

## Journey Stages

**Search → Check Availability → Login → Select Train → Passenger Details → Payment → Confirmation → After Booking**

---

## Journey Breakdown

| Stage | User Goal | Pain Point | Opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Search | Find a suitable train | Slow loading | Simple and responsive search |
| Check Availability | Understand available options | Unclear availability | Clear seat and fare information |
| Login | Access booking | OTP/login friction | Simpler authentication |
| Select Train | Choose a train | Too much information | Clear comparison |
| Passenger Details | Enter passenger information | Form friction | Simple form |
| Payment | Complete transaction | Payment uncertainty | Clear payment status |
| Confirmation | Know booking result | Unclear status | Transparent confirmation |
| After Booking | Manage journey | Information can feel scattered | Journey dashboard |

---

## Emotional Journey

**Prepared → Focused → Stressed → Uncertain → Relief**

The most significant uncertainty occurs around authentication, payment and booking confirmation.

---

## Key Opportunity

Reduce uncertainty during the most important moments of the railway booking journey.

The experience should clearly communicate:

**What happened → What is happening → What should I do next?**

---

## Product Principle

> **Never leave the passenger wondering what happened.**

Every critical state should answer:

1. What happened?
2. What is happening?
3. What should I do next?

---

## Product Opportunity

The journey suggests four important areas for improvement:

- Reliability
- Transparency
- Simplicity
- Error recovery
