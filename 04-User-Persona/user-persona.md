# 🚆 RailEase — User Persona

## Proposed User Persona

### Rahul Sharma

**Age:** 28  
**Occupation:** Working Professional  
**Location:** Delhi NCR

---

## Background

Rahul frequently travels long-distance by train and occasionally books overnight journeys.

He usually books tickets online and uses Tatkal when planned tickets are unavailable.

---

## Goals

- Book tickets quickly
- Find the right train
- Understand seat availability
- Complete payment successfully
- Know whether the ticket is confirmed
- Avoid login and OTP problems

---

## Frustrations

- Slow loading
- Server-related issues
- Tatkal booking pressure
- Unexpected logout
- OTP delays
- Unclear payment status
- Confusing error messages

---

## Behaviour

- Books railway tickets online
- Checks availability before booking
- Uses Tatkal when required
- Compares different trains
- Prefers a simple and predictable booking process

---

## Needs

- Speed
- Reliability
- Clear availability
- Simple authentication
- Transparent booking status
- Helpful error recovery

---

## Quote

> "I just want to know whether my ticket is booked or not."

---

## Core Need

Rahul needs a fast, reliable and transparent booking experience without unnecessary uncertainty.

---

## Research Basis

This persona is a fictional representation based on the initial research themes identified during the case study.

It is not based on a single individual and should be validated and refined through further user research.
