# 🚆 RailEase — User Research Findings

## Research Objective

The objective of this research is to understand the problems users face while booking and travelling by train in India.

The research focuses on:

- Train ticket booking
- Tatkal booking
- Website/app usability
- Login and OTP experience
- Booking reliability
- Train travel experience
- Features users would find useful

## Research Method

Primary research was conducted through a Google Forms survey.

Secondary research was conducted using publicly available user experiences, reviews, and official railway service information.
## Initial Responses

The survey currently has 6 responses.

> Note: These are early findings and may change as more responses are collected.

## Key Findings

### 1. Long-Distance Travel

All 6 respondents reported that they usually take long-distance train journeys.

### 2. Booking Platforms

Most respondents use the IRCTC website and/or IRCTC Rail Connect app to book tickets.

### 3. Booking Experience

Respondents generally rated the current booking experience around 3–4 out of 5.

This suggests that the experience is not completely unusable, but there are significant points of friction.

### 4. Common Problems

The most frequently reported problems include:

- Slow loading
- Website/app unavailable
- Server busy
- Confusing navigation
- Login/logout issues
- OTP-related friction
- Errors during the booking process

### 5. Tatkal Booking

Several respondents have attempted Tatkal booking.

Common problems reported during Tatkal include:

- Server busy
- Website/app becoming unavailable
- Slow loading
- Maintenance-related issues

### 6. User Expectations

Users indicated that they would like improvements to the existing website/app experience, particularly around:

- Speed
- Reliability
- Easier navigation
- Better availability during high-demand periods
- Clearer error handling

## Initial Problem Hypotheses

Based on the early responses, the following problems require further investigation:

1. **Performance:** Users may struggle with slow loading and unavailable services.
2. **Reliability:** Users may lose confidence when the platform fails during important booking moments.
3. **Usability:** Navigation and the booking flow may create unnecessary friction.
4. **Authentication:** Login and OTP issues may interrupt the booking journey.
5. **Tatkal Experience:** High demand may make the booking experience particularly stressful.

## Important Insight

The research suggests that the opportunity may not simply be to create a visually better railway app.

A stronger opportunity may be to create a railway experience that is:

**Fast + Reliable + Simple + Transparent**

However, more research is required before selecting the final problem to solve.

## Next Steps

- Continue collecting survey responses
- Identify recurring problems
- Segment users based on travel and booking behaviour
- Prioritize problems based on frequency and impact
- Define the final problem statement
