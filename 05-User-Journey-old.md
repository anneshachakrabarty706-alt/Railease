# 🚆 IRCTC Case Study — User Journey Map

## Persona

### Rahul Sharma

Rahul is a working professional who frequently travels long-distance by train and uses IRCTC for online ticket booking.

---

## Journey Goal

Rahul wants to book a train ticket quickly and confidently without facing technical issues or uncertainty about his booking status.

---

## User Journey

| Stage | User Action | User Thought | Pain Point | Opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Search | Searches for trains and enters journey details | "Which train should I choose?" | Too much information can make comparison difficult | Make train information easier to compare |
| 2. Check Availability | Checks seats, class and fare | "Will I get a confirmed seat?" | Seat availability can change quickly | Show availability and status clearly |
| 3. Login | Logs into the account | "I hope I don't get logged out." | Login, OTP and authentication issues | Make authentication faster and more reliable |
| 4. Select Train | Selects train and class | "I need to book this quickly." | Slow loading and server issues | Improve speed and system reliability |
| 5. Enter Details | Adds passenger information | "I don't want to lose my seat." | Limited time and booking pressure | Simplify passenger details |
| 6. Payment | Makes the payment | "Did my payment go through?" | Payment/booking uncertainty | Provide clear payment and booking status |
| 7. Confirmation | Waits for booking confirmation | "Is my ticket actually booked?" | Unclear status can create anxiety | Show clear confirmation or next steps |
| 8. After Booking | Checks ticket/PNR details | "Where can I find everything?" | Important information may require navigation | Create a simple journey dashboard |

---

## Key Pain Points

The most important pain points identified from the initial research are:

### 🔴 1. Slow Loading

Users frequently reported slow loading during the booking experience.

### 🔴 2. Server / Website Availability

Users reported situations where the website or app becomes unavailable or shows server-related problems.

### 🔴 3. Tatkal Booking Pressure

Tatkal booking creates additional pressure because users have limited time and limited seat availability.

### 🟠 4. Login and OTP Friction

Login, OTP and unexpected logout issues can interrupt the booking journey.

### 🟠 5. Payment and Booking Uncertainty

Users need clear information about whether their payment and ticket booking were successful.

---

## User Emotional Journey

### Before Booking
**😊 Prepared → 😐 Focused**

Rahul knows what he wants but expects the booking process to be quick.

### During Booking
**😐 Focused → 😟 Stressed**

Slow loading, server issues and limited availability increase pressure.

### During Payment
**😟 Stressed → 😰 Uncertain**

Rahul wants confirmation that his money and booking are safe.

### After Booking
**😌 Relief**

A clear confirmation gives Rahul confidence that his journey is secured.

---

## Biggest Opportunity

The biggest opportunity is to reduce uncertainty throughout the railway booking journey.

RailEase should help users understand:

**What happened → What is happening → What should I do next?**

---

## Product Principle

> **Never leave the passenger wondering what happened.**

This principle can guide the design of important experiences such as:

- Ticket booking
- Payment
- Tatkal booking
- Login
- Failed transactions
- Refunds
- Train status
- Food orders
