# 🚆 RailEase — Problem Prioritization

## Objective

The objective of this prioritization exercise is to identify which user problem RailEase should focus on solving first.

The problems were identified from the initial user research survey.

---

## Identified Problems

The research highlighted the following recurring problems:

1. Slow loading
2. Website/app unavailable
3. Server busy during high-demand periods
4. Login and OTP issues
5. Unexpected logout
6. Seat availability issues
7. Payment and booking-related problems
8. Confusing navigation

---

## Prioritization Framework

Problems were evaluated using three factors:

### Frequency
How often users reported experiencing the problem.

### User Impact
How seriously the problem affects the user's journey.

### Opportunity
How valuable it could be to improve the experience.

Each factor is scored from 1 to 5.

- 1 = Low
- 2 = Slightly Low
- 3 = Medium
- 4 = High
- 5 = Very High

Priority Score = Frequency × User Impact × Opportunity

# Priority Areas

## 🔴 P0 — Booking Reliability & Performance

### Why?

Slow loading, server issues and website/app availability can prevent users from completing an important task: booking a ticket.

These issues become particularly important during high-demand periods such as Tatkal booking.

---

## 🔴 P0 — Tatkal Booking Experience

### Why?

Tatkal users have limited time and limited seat availability.

Technical problems such as slow loading, server issues or booking failures can make the experience highly stressful.

---

## 🟠 P1 — Payment & Booking Transparency

### Why?

When users are unsure whether a payment or booking has succeeded, they may worry about losing money or having to repeat the transaction.

RailEase should clearly communicate:

**What happened → Current status → What to do next**

---

## 🟠 P1 — Login & Authentication

### Why?

Login, OTP and unexpected logout issues can interrupt users during the booking journey.

Authentication should be quick, reliable and easy to recover from.

---

## 🟡 P2 — Navigation & Usability

### Why?

Confusing navigation creates friction, but the research currently suggests that reliability and booking performance are more important problems to solve first.

---

# Initial Product Opportunity

Based on the research so far, RailEase should explore:

> **Creating a simpler, more reliable and transparent railway booking experience, especially during high-demand booking situations.**

The product should reduce uncertainty and clearly communicate the status of important actions such as:

- Searching for trains
- Checking availability
- Booking tickets
- Making payments
- Confirming bookings
- Handling failures
- Managing refunds
