# 🚆 RailEase — Success Metrics

## Objective

Measure whether RailEase makes railway booking more reliable, transparent and easier for passengers.

---

## Primary Metrics

### 1. Booking Completion Rate

Percentage of users who successfully complete a booking after starting the booking flow.

### 2. Booking Failure Rate

Percentage of booking attempts that fail before confirmation.

### 3. Payment-to-Confirmation Drop-off

Percentage of users who make a payment but do not reach booking confirmation.

### 4. Error Recovery Rate

Percentage of users who successfully recover after encountering an error.

### 5. Task Completion Time

Time taken by a user to complete the booking journey.

### 6. User Satisfaction

User-reported satisfaction after completing the booking task.

---

## Secondary Metrics

- Repeated booking attempts
- Support/help requests
- Login/OTP abandonment
- Error frequency
- User-reported confusion

---

## North Star Metric

### Successful Booking Completion Rate

The core goal is to help passengers successfully complete their booking with less uncertainty and friction.

---

## Measurement Principle

Metrics should be used to understand whether the product is solving the user problem, not simply whether users are interacting with more features.
