# 🚆 RailEase — Product Roadmap

## Roadmap Objective

Build RailEase in stages, starting with the core railway booking experience and gradually expanding into supporting journey features.

---

## Phase 1 — MVP

### Focus: Reliable Booking

- Simplified booking flow
- Clear booking & payment status
- Better error recovery
- Basic authentication recovery

**Goal:** Help users complete bookings with less confusion and uncertainty.

---

## Phase 2 — Booking Experience

### Focus: High-Demand Booking

- Tatkal-ready booking experience
- Improved availability visibility
- Faster recovery from failed attempts
- Better login and OTP experience

**Goal:** Reduce friction during high-demand booking situations.

---

## Phase 3 — Journey Management

### Focus: Complete Journey Experience

- My Journeys dashboard
- Upcoming journey information
- Ticket management
- Journey status updates
- Important travel notifications

**Goal:** Make RailEase useful beyond the initial booking.

---

## Phase 4 — Future Opportunities

### Focus: Supporting Travel Needs

- Food ordering experience
- Food order tracking
- Refund and complaint status
- Personalized journey assistance
- Additional railway travel services

**Goal:** Create a more connected railway travel experience.

---

## Prioritization Principle

RailEase will prioritize features based on:

**User Impact + User Need + Frequency + Feasibility**

The product will focus on solving the most important booking problems before expanding into additional features.
