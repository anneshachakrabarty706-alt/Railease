# 🚆 IRCTC Case Study — Final Problem Statement

## Background

IRCTC is widely used by passengers to search for trains, check availability and book railway tickets online.

However, the booking experience can become difficult and stressful when users encounter slow loading, server issues, authentication problems or uncertainty during payment and booking.

These issues become particularly important during high-demand periods such as Tatkal booking.

---

## Research Insight

Initial user research indicates that users experience multiple points of friction during the railway booking journey.

The most significant themes identified so far are:

- Slow loading
- Website/app availability issues
- Server-related problems
- Login and OTP friction
- Unexpected logout
- Tatkal booking difficulties
- Payment and booking uncertainty
- Confusing navigation

The research currently suggests that reliability and transparency are more important opportunities than visual redesign alone.

---

## User Problem

Passengers need a reliable and transparent way to complete railway bookings because technical issues, authentication friction and unclear booking status can create stress and uncertainty during an already time-sensitive process.

---

## Final Problem Statement

> **How might we make the railway booking experience more reliable, transparent and stress-free for passengers, especially during high-demand booking situations such as Tatkal?**

---

## Why This Problem Matters

Booking a train ticket is an important task for passengers.

When the booking experience becomes slow, unavailable or unclear, users may:

- Lose valuable time
- Miss available seats
- Repeat booking attempts
- Become uncertain about payment status
- Experience unnecessary stress
- Lose confidence in the platform

A better experience should help users understand what is happening at every important step.

---

## Product Opportunity

The opportunity for RailEase is to explore a railway booking experience that prioritizes:

### ⚡ Speed
Reduce unnecessary friction and waiting.

### 🔒 Reliability
Help users complete important actions with fewer interruptions.

### 👁️ Transparency
Clearly communicate booking, payment and transaction status.

### 🧭 Simplicity
Make the booking journey easier to understand and navigate.

### 🆘 Recovery
Give users clear next steps when something goes wrong.

---

## Product Principle

> **Never leave the passenger wondering what happened.**

The experience should clearly communicate:

**What happened → What is happening → What should I do next?**

---

## Research Status

This problem statement is based on the initial user research and prioritization conducted for this case study.

The research will continue as more survey responses are collected.

Future research may validate, refine or change the problem statement.
